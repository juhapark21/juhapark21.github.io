So how should I learn?

You can learn in any way that suits you, with one exception - DO NOT LEARN VISUALLY. (no charts, associating dots/dashes to certain images, etc.)

Learning visually will limit your speed at a certain point and likely hinder your progress.


Here are some resources for learning CW (mostly radio-related):

  • LCWO.net (free website)
  • Long Island CW Club (structured classes, free for kids!)
  • Kurt Zoglmann's channel (good practice audio, progressively harder lessons)
  • Morse Code Ninja (learning/practice website)
  • Morse Walker (for more advanced - practice ham radio QSOs with simulated pileups/QRM)

These are just the ones I have personally used. There are so many more resource out there and I encourage you to explore!
